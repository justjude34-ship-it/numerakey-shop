How to use this budget tracker (beginner friendly)

  1. Choose a template from the dropdown (Monthly is a great start).
  2. Edit the Period label so you know which month or year this is for.
  3. In the Income section, type your sources of money and amounts. Click “+ Add Income” for more rows.
  4. In the Expenses section, pick a category (or type a new one), choose a colour, and enter the amount. The pie chart updates automatically.
  5. Watch the Summary cards: they calculate totals, remaining money, and percentage spent for you.
  6. Everything auto-saves in your browser. You can also download CSV or PDF anytime.

Keyboard shortcuts: Ctrl + S force save · Ctrl + E export CSV · Tab move between fields · + (when focused on a table) add a row
