If and when you choose to leave, use the Escape tab and call a service to make a plan that fits your life. Leaving can raise risk. You do not have to do it alone.

The cycle

Idealize (love bombing) → Devalue → Discard → Hoover. The good version is used to hook you. The cruel version is used to control you. The return is used to pull you back.

1. Idealize

Intense attention, soulmate talk, future plans, gifts. It moves too fast on purpose.

2. Devalue

Criticism, contempt, silence, moving goalposts. You chase the person you met at the start.

3. Discard

Sudden coldness, replacement, blame. You are treated as disposable.

4. Hoover

Sorry, I have changed, the kids, one conversation. It targets hope. Watch actions for a long time, not words.

Grey rock

Be boring. Short. No emotion. No extra story. This is short-term, when you cannot go quiet yet. Not a life plan.

Read this first

The most dangerous time can be when you leave or when they find out you plan to. Do not announce the plan. If it is safe, build it with 1800RESPECT or the NSW DV Line. This page is a checklist, not a personal safety plan.

Money and housing

  • Leaving Violence Program — up to $5,155 and 12 weeks of support. Any gender. Visa holders can apply. leavingviolenceprogram.org.au or call 1800 253 283.
  • NSW DV Line can help with crisis accommodation: 1800 656 463.
  • Link2Home housing: 1800 152 152.
  • Centrelink Crisis Payment and a social worker at Services Australia.
  • National Debt Helpline 1800 880 052.
  • In NSW you may be able to end a tenancy immediately for domestic violence, with no break fee. Ask Fair Trading 13 32 20 or a local tenants service via tenants.org.au.

Pets

People delay leaving because of animals. That is common. Plan the animals as part of the exit, not after it.

  • RSPCA NSW Domestic and Family Violence Support can board or foster while you get safe.
  • Weekdays 02 9782 4408. After hours 02 9770 7555.
  • Pack food, medication, lead or carrier, and microchip or vet papers if you can.
  • An AVO can include harm to animals.

Legal doors in NSW

  • Police can apply for an ADVO for you. You can also apply at a Local Court. No filing fee.
  • Legal Aid NSW Domestic Violence Unit 1800 979 529.
  • Women’s Domestic Violence Court Advocacy Service 1800 938 227.
  • Women’s Legal Service NSW 02 8745 6988 or rural 1800 810 784.
  • Coercive control is a crime in NSW. A pattern of abuse against a current or former intimate partner can be charged. You do not have to name the law. Tell police what has been happening.

Day of leaving

  • Take people and animals first. Possessions can wait.
  • Leave when the risk is lower if you have a choice. A calm window is often safer than a fight.
  • Have a place to go that they do not control.
  • Use a code word with one trusted person.
  • If you are in danger now, call 000.

After you leave

The pull-back is common. Missing them does not mean it was safe. The bond is often a trauma bond, not proof they changed.

First days

Keep contact narrow. Do not debate the past. Eat, sleep, tell one safe person.

If they say they have changed

That is usually hoovering. Change is shown over a long time with no pressure. You do not have to test it.

Smear and flying monkeys

They may tell a story first. Document facts. Tell safe people once. Do not chase every rumour. Do not explain your case to their messengers.

Kids

Do not badmouth. Stay steady. Document put-downs. Your calm presence is the counter.

Healing is not

Convincing them. Winning the story with mutual friends. Going back to check if this time is different.
