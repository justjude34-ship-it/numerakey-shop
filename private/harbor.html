Energy banking

Energy is a budget, not a moral score. Recovery is part of the plan.

  • Put high-value tasks in your best hours.
  • Schedule rest before you crash, not after.
  • Cool your body if heat drains you.
  • One task at a time when cog fog is high.
  • Ask for help on the heavy lifts.

When to get medical help

This is a companion — not emergency care.

Contact your neurology team for new or worsening symptoms lasting more than a day, especially walking, vision, speech, or bladder.

Seek emergency care for sudden severe symptoms, stroke-like signs, chest pain, or trouble breathing. In Australia, Lifeline is 13 11 14. In the US, call 988.
