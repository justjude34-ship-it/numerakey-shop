Guide

Vesper is a private sound studio. Two exclusive modes. Never mixed. No account. No tracking. Works offline from this file.

How to listen

Choose one mode, sit with it, and stop when the timer ends or when you have had enough. Start a little quieter than you think you want, then raise it. The default is already strong.

Binaural beats

One ear hears a carrier. The other hears that carrier plus a small offset. The difference is the beat. Headphones are required — speakers collapse the field. Each ear is a harmonic stack, not a thin sine.

Delta sits near sleep. Theta is inward. Alpha is open attention. Beta is desk work. Gamma is a short, bright field.

Tibetan bronze bowls

Synthesized struck and sung bronze: inharmonic partials, a soft mallet, long decay. Speakers or headphones both work. Strike is sparse. Ritual leaves space. Bath lets rings overlap. Sustain sings one bowl.

Why the modes stay exclusive

Binaural beats need a clean stereo field. A bowl ringing in both ears fills that field and the beat disappears. Bronze wants air and decay. A carrier sitting under it turns the metal into mud. Journeys follow the same rule.

Safety

Not a medical device. Do not use binaural beats while driving. If you have a history of seizures or have been told to avoid rhythmic sound, skip the binaural studio. Stop if you feel uneasy.
