How this spreadsheet works

  1. You are looking at a demo first. Wander around. The sample pay and bills are not yours.
  2. When you are ready, tap Keep these numbers as mine or Start a blank budget.
  3. Income is money coming in. Expenses: Budgeted is the plan, Actual is what you really spent.
  4. Each expense has a colour. That colour is its slice on the pie chart.
  5. The summary updates as you type. Green remaining means you spent less than your income.
  6. It auto-saves on this computer. CSV and PDF are yours to keep. No subscription.

Shortcuts: Ctrl+S or ⌘S reminds you it is saved · Ctrl+E or ⌘E downloads CSV · Tab moves between cells
