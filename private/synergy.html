What This Reading Covers

Enter each person's full birth name and date of birth. The reading draws on two complementary branches of Pythagorean numerology:

  • Name numbers — Expression, Soul Urge, and Personality — calculated from the letters of the birth name, in the tradition of numerologist Matthew Goodwin.
  • Birth-date numbers — Life Path, Birthday, Attitude, Personal Year, and the Arrows of Pythagoras birth grid — calculated from the date of birth, in the tradition of numerologist David A. Phillips.

Together they're woven into a compatibility score, a category breakdown, and a full written reading you can save as a keepsake PDF.

All interpretations here are original write-ups composed for this app in the spirit of those two traditions — offered for reflection and entertainment, not as a substitute for either author's published work.
