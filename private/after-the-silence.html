A note on sides

Estrangement is rarely a cartoon. This companion will not tell you the other person is a villain, and it will not tell you that you deserved the cutoff. It will help you keep a life while the story is unfinished.

About

After the Silence is an original private companion for parents living with adult-child estrangement.

It was inspired by the same problem many parents carry after reading books such as Sheri McGregor’s Done With The Crying — reclaiming a life whether or not reconciliation arrives. It is not an official companion, not a digitised workbook, and not affiliated with the author or publisher.

Buy once. Yours to keep. Everything you write stays in this browser on this device. There is no account and no feed.

This is not therapy, legal advice, or a crisis service. Version 1.0
