Both systems
Business names

The one number
you get to choose

Your birth date is fixed and your name was chosen for you. A trading name is neither — which makes it the most practical thing in the whole system, and the reason this section exists.

Numera runs the same method over the name above your door. It asks whether the number suits the work — a 4 on a building firm, a 3 on a studio — whether it suits you, the person who has to run it every day, and what the name leaves empty, because an unfilled square shows up as the thing that always needs chasing.

A well-chosen name will not make a business succeed. It removes friction, which is worth having and is all that is claimed.

How it works

Three minutes,
then hours of reading

Numera is built to be read rather than skimmed. The calculation takes a moment; the interpretation is long on purpose, because a chart described in six words is not a chart, it is a horoscope.

There is a full course inside it as well — every part of both systems taught on its own, so you can eventually read any chart, not only your own.

Plainly stated

What this is, and what it isn't

Symbolic, not scientific

Numerology is a symbolic system with a long history and no empirical basis. Nothing here predicts events. It is a way of describing a temperament, and it is useful in the way any good description is useful.

Not advice

Nothing in Numera substitutes for medical, legal, financial or psychological advice. Where the older books discuss health, that material is given as historical tradition and clearly marked as such.

Your details stay yours

Everything is worked out in your own browser. Names and dates are held on your device and are never sent anywhere, because there is nowhere to send them.

Written from scratch

The methods are Phillips's and Goodwin's. Every word of interpretation is original writing — no text from either author's books is reproduced.
