How to use this

Open a rune. Read the name, the sound, and what the old poems actually say. Then read the everyday example.

An ætt is a family of eight runes. Merkstave is a modern study habit: reading a rune as if it were turned over. The old poems do not do that. If a rune looks the same upside down, this app says so. It does not invent a reverse.
