Reach a person

This is the part I can't do

Sanctuary works offline, which means it can't call anyone or fetch a number for you. A human can do what this app cannot.

If you are in danger right now

Call your local emergency number. If speaking isn't safe, many countries let you signal silently to an emergency operator — it's worth knowing how that works where you live before you need it.

If you're not safe from yourself

Please tell one person tonight. A crisis line, your doctor, a friend, a family member, a neighbour — whoever you'd reach first. Free crisis lines run 24 hours in most countries; searching for the one where you live takes under a minute, and someone you trust can look it up with you.

Telling someone is not a burden or an overreaction. It's the step that changes what happens next.

Worth doing when things are calmer

Write your emergency number, one crisis line and two people you'd call on paper, and keep it somewhere you'll find it fast. Sanctuary deliberately doesn't store those names — a written note is safer and it works when a phone doesn't.

Aria can sit with you, but she isn't a person, a counsellor or a service. Nothing here replaces talking to someone real.

Privacy

How this works

It never goes online

Sanctuary is one file on your device. It makes no network requests, has no account, no analytics and no sync. Aria's replies are written into the app itself, which is why she can answer with the internet off.

What is stored, and where

Your journal entries, mood check-ins, saved lists and settings are kept in this browser's local storage on this device only. Your conversation with Aria is never stored — it clears when you close the app.

Quick exit

The button in the corner, or the Escape key, instantly covers Sanctuary with a plain notes list and clears your conversation. Tap the faint line at the bottom of that list to come back. If your browser saves history, clearing it separately is worth doing.

Erase everything

This removes your journal, PIN, check-ins and lists from this device. It cannot be undone.

Sanctuary is a self-help companion. It isn't therapy, diagnosis, or an emergency service, and it can't tell how serious things are for you. If you're struggling, a real person is the next step.
